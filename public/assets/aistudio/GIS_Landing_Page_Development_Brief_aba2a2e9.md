# Global Institute of Studies (GIS), Nilai --- Landing Page Development Brief

## 1. Project overview

Develop a polished, responsive, lead-generation landing page for
**Global Institute of Studies (GIS), Nilai, Negeri Sembilan, Malaysia**.

The primary audience is parents and guardians of teenagers aged **15 and
above** who are considering post-secondary, diploma, TVET, skills-based
or other further-education pathways for their children.

The website should help parents understand how GIS supports students in
four important areas:

1.  Academic guidance and learning support.
2.  Financial assistance and education funding.
3.  Student welfare, supervision and safety.
4.  Industry relationships and career pathways after graduation.

The page should build trust, answer parents' concerns and encourage them
to contact GIS for programme information or counselling.

## 2. Core design direction

### Brand feel

-   Professional, welcoming, reassuring and modern.
-   Malaysian in its people, photography, language and cultural details.
-   Premium but approachable; avoid an overly corporate or impersonal
    feel.
-   Use clear, parent-friendly language and concise calls to action.

### Colour palette

  Purpose                Colour           Suggested value
  ---------------------- ---------------- -----------------
  Primary                Deep navy blue   `#102C46`
  Accent                 Warm gold        `#D5A64B`
  Main background        White            `#FFFFFF`
  Alternate background   Warm ivory       `#F4F1EA`
  Supporting accent      Muted sage       `#547A70`
  Body text              Deep charcoal    `#263442`

Use navy for headings, navigation and important buttons; gold for
highlights and calls to action; white and ivory for the page
backgrounds.

### Typography

Use a modern, highly legible sans-serif pairing, for example:

-   **Headings:** Poppins, or a similar geometric sans-serif.
-   **Body text:** Inter, or a similar readable sans-serif.

Maintain clear heading hierarchy, comfortable line spacing and strong
contrast.

### Theme

-   Light theme only.
-   Do not provide a dark-mode toggle.
-   Do not automatically switch to dark mode based on the visitor's
    device preference.
-   Keep all sections bright, readable and consistent.

## 3. Photography and image direction

Use authentic-looking, high-quality photographs with a strong Malaysian
Malay identity.

### People and clothing

Show Malaysian Malay people in natural, credible education and family
situations:

-   Parents in modest, contemporary Malaysian clothing.
-   Mothers and female students wearing hijab where appropriate.
-   Fathers and male students wearing suitable smart-casual clothing,
    baju Melayu or programme-appropriate attire.
-   Students, lecturers, mentors, wardens and graduates who look like
    people in a Malaysian education setting.
-   Clothing must be modest, neat, practical and appropriate to the
    activity.

### Required image subjects

1.  **Hero banner:** A Malay family with a teenage student at or near a
    recognisable GIS-style campus setting. The student should be the
    visual focus, with supportive parents.
2.  **Academic support:** A Malay lecturer or mentor guiding students
    through coursework.
3.  **Financial assistance:** A parent and student discussing education
    options or documents with a counsellor.
4.  **Student welfare:** A suitably dressed warden or student-support
    staff member interacting with students in a professional, reassuring
    way.
5.  **Career pathways:** Students undertaking practical training,
    workshop activities or industry-related learning.
6.  **Programme cards:** Relevant imagery for culinary and hospitality,
    automotive, welding and electrical, early childhood education,
    healthcare and elderly care, and logistics or maritime.

Use genuine GIS photography wherever possible. Generated or stock
imagery may be used for the initial prototype, but must not be presented
as documentary proof of GIS facilities, staff, students, partnerships or
outcomes. Replace illustrative images with approved GIS photographs
before public launch.

### Image treatment

-   Use wide landscape crops for the hero and major content panels.
-   Use consistent aspect ratios for programme cards.
-   Prefer natural daylight, warm expressions and realistic Malaysian
    settings.
-   Avoid culturally inappropriate clothing, foreign-looking campus
    environments, exaggerated uniforms, distorted hands, fabricated
    signage or inaccurate logos.
-   Add descriptive alt text to meaningful images.

## 4. Page structure and content requirements

Build the page in the following order.

### A. Header and navigation

Include:

-   GIS logo on the left.
-   Navigation links: Home, About GIS, Programmes, Student Support, Why
    GIS and FAQ.
-   A prominent WhatsApp contact button.
-   A mobile navigation menu with accessible keyboard and screen-reader
    behaviour.

Use a sticky header if it does not obscure page content.

### B. Cinematic widescreen hero banner

The hero must be a visually strong, full-width, widescreen banner.

Requirements:

-   Target a cinematic 16:9 visual composition on desktop.
-   Use a high-resolution campus-and-family image.
-   Keep the main family/student subjects visible at common desktop and
    mobile crops.
-   Add a subtle navy gradient overlay behind text where needed for
    readability.
-   Avoid excessive visual clutter.

Suggested headline:

**Your Child's Future. Our Commitment.**

Suggested supporting copy:

"Quality education, practical skills and real career opportunities. At
GIS, we understand what matters most to parents. We provide support to
help your teenager progress academically, financially, safely and in
their career journey."

Primary CTA: **Explore GIS Programmes**

Secondary CTA: **Speak to Our Team**

Include a small note that the information is intended for parents of
teenagers aged 15 and above, subject to each programme's actual entry
requirements.

### C. Four parent commitments

Present four clear cards or icon-led panels:

1.  **Academic Support** --- Mentor-Mentee Programme for students with
    different learning needs and levels of ability.
2.  **Financial Assistance** --- Information and guidance on PTPTN,
    PTPK, KWSP, Baitulmal and other applicable assistance.
3.  **Student Safety** --- Warden support, student welfare and the
    stated 24-hour contact-response arrangement.
4.  **Career Opportunities** --- Industry relationships and Memorandums
    of Understanding (MOUs) that may provide industry exposure and
    employment pathways.

Each card should link to its corresponding detailed section.

### D. Academic support section

Suggested heading:

**Every Student Has Potential. We Help Them Move Forward.**

Explain the GIS Mentor-Mentee Programme in clear, supportive language.

Content points:

-   Students have different strengths, learning speeds and support
    needs.
-   Mentors provide guidance and encouragement.
-   Students who need additional help can receive appropriate guidance.
-   Students with strong potential can also be encouraged to develop
    further.
-   The aim is to support student progress and confidence.

Do not promise guaranteed grades, graduation or academic outcomes unless
GIS has an approved, documented basis for making that claim.

### E. Financial assistance section

Suggested heading:

**Don't Let Financial Questions Stand Between Your Child and Further
Education.**

Explain that GIS can guide parents on applicable financial-assistance
routes, including:

-   PTPTN
-   PTPK
-   KWSP
-   Baitulmal
-   Other assistance options, where applicable

Provide a CTA such as **Speak to Us About Education Financing**.

Important: Do not imply that every student qualifies for every funding
scheme. Eligibility, approved programmes, funding limits and application
processes must be confirmed with the relevant provider and GIS before
publication. Do not invent scholarships, discounts, instalment plans or
guaranteed funding.

### F. Student safety and welfare section

Suggested heading:

**Your Child May Be Away from Home, But They Should Not Feel Alone.**

Explain only the arrangements GIS can verify, such as:

-   Warden supervision and support.
-   Student welfare guidance.
-   A parent/student contact channel with a stated 24-hour response
    arrangement.
-   Support for students adjusting to campus or hostel life.

Do not imply that a warden is physically on duty 24 hours a day unless
GIS confirms this. Do not claim CCTV coverage, medical facilities,
security systems or other infrastructure unless verified.

### G. Career opportunities and industry relationships

Suggested heading:

**Preparing Students for the World of Work.**

Explain GIS's industry relationships and MOUs accurately.

Content points may include:

-   Industry-informed practical learning, where applicable.
-   Exposure to relevant work environments.
-   Industry connections through documented MOUs.
-   Guidance on potential employment pathways.

Do not describe an MOU as a guaranteed job placement. Avoid guaranteed
employment, salary or job-placement-rate claims unless GIS has approved,
verifiable evidence and the claim is legally appropriate.

### H. Programmes and courses

Use an image-led card grid or horizontal carousel. The initial visual
categories may include:

-   Culinary Arts and Hospitality
-   Automotive
-   Welding and Electrical
-   Early Childhood Education
-   Healthcare and Elderly Care
-   Logistics, Transport and Maritime

Each card should have a relevant image, programme title, a concise
description and a **Learn More** link.

**Critical content check:** The categories above are design
placeholders, not confirmation that every programme is currently
offered. Before publishing, GIS must supply and approve the exact active
programme list, official titles, qualification level, accreditation
status, intake availability, duration, entry requirements, minimum age
and application details. Do not invent course names or display
unverified accreditation logos.

### I. Why choose GIS

Create a concise trust-building section using only verified strengths,
such as:

-   Experienced lecturers and trainers.
-   Practical learning.
-   Student guidance and support.
-   Industry relationships.
-   A supportive learning environment.

Confirm every statement with GIS before publication.

### J. Parent/student testimonials

Include a testimonial carousel only if GIS has permission to publish the
statements and the people quoted have consented.

-   Use real, approved testimonials.
-   Identify the speaker accurately and obtain consent for names and
    photographs.
-   Do not fabricate parent quotes, student success stories or graduate
    outcomes.
-   If no approved testimonials are available, omit the section or use a
    clearly labelled general information panel.

### K. Frequently asked questions

Create an accessible accordion with questions such as:

-   What are the entry requirements?
-   Can students aged 15 and above apply?
-   What programmes are available?
-   Can students apply for PTPTN or PTPK?
-   What financial assistance may be available?
-   What student or hostel support is provided?
-   What career pathways are available after graduation?
-   How can parents arrange a campus visit or speak with a counsellor?

Answers must be checked and approved by GIS. Link to the relevant
programme or enquiry section where useful.

### L. Final enquiry and lead form

Use a prominent, friendly final CTA, for example:

**Let's Talk About Your Child's Future.**

Form fields:

-   Full Name --- required.
-   Phone Number --- required.
-   Email Address --- required.

Add a clear consent statement explaining that the visitor's information
will be used by GIS to respond to the enquiry and handled according to
the published privacy notice.

On successful form submission:

1.  Validate the required fields on the client and server sides.
2.  Submit the enquiry to a Google Apps Script web app connected to a
    Google Sheet owned by the site owner's Google account.
3.  Record the submission date/time, name, phone, email, enquiry source
    and follow-up status.
4.  Show a clear success or error message.
5.  Open WhatsApp with the enquiry message pre-filled.

Use this exact WhatsApp message template:

> Hi, I am \[name\] and interested in GIS offerings. This is my \[phone
> number\] and \[email\]. Please contact me and provide further
> informations.

The visitor must manually press **Send** in WhatsApp. Do not claim that
the website can send a WhatsApp message silently or automatically.

### M. Footer

Include:

-   GIS logo.
-   Official address and contact details, once supplied and verified.
-   Navigation links.
-   Privacy Policy.
-   Terms and Conditions.
-   Official social-media links.
-   Copyright notice.

Do not invent social-media accounts, addresses, telephone numbers or
legal policy text.

## 5. Motion and interaction design

Use subtle, polished motion to make the page feel contemporary without
distracting from the information.

Required effects:

-   Smooth scrolling for in-page navigation.
-   Gentle fade-and-rise reveal as sections enter the viewport.
-   Staggered entrance for cards and icon groups.
-   Short text transitions in selected headings or hero content.
-   Subtle image reveal or scale effect on section entry.
-   Hover and focus states for buttons, links and cards.
-   Smooth accordion opening and closing.
-   Optional carousel movement for programme cards or approved
    testimonials.

Motion guidelines:

-   Keep animations short and restrained.
-   Avoid continuous movement that competes with reading.
-   Do not make essential information available only through animation.
-   Respect the visitor's `prefers-reduced-motion` setting by disabling
    or simplifying non-essential motion.
-   Ensure animations do not cause layout shifts or reduce
    accessibility.

## 6. Responsive behaviour

The landing page must work well on:

-   Large desktop screens.
-   Standard laptops.
-   Tablets.
-   Mobile phones.

Mobile requirements:

-   Stack content into a clear single-column layout.
-   Keep text readable without horizontal scrolling.
-   Reposition or crop the hero image so the family/student remains
    visible.
-   Use touch-friendly buttons and form controls.
-   Make the WhatsApp CTA easy to reach without covering important
    content.
-   Collapse navigation into an accessible menu.
-   Ensure programme cards can be browsed comfortably on touch screens.

## 8. Google Sheets lead capture

The intended spreadsheet should be owned by the user's Google account.
The developer must not assume that the account used to sign in to
ChatGPT is automatically available to the website or development
environment.

Recommended implementation:

1.  The owner creates or authorises a Google Sheet using their own
    Google account.
2.  Create a Google Apps Script project connected to that spreadsheet.
3.  Deploy the Apps Script as a Web App with the minimum appropriate
    access settings.
4.  Configure the landing page with the deployed Web App URL.
5.  Submit validated form data to the Apps Script endpoint.
6.  Validate and sanitise incoming values in Apps Script before writing
    a new row.
7.  Return a success or error result that the frontend can handle.
8.  Test the complete flow using a non-sensitive test enquiry before
    launch.

Suggested spreadsheet columns:

  Column                Purpose
  --------------------- -----------------------------------
  Submitted At          Date and time of enquiry
  Full Name             Visitor's name
  Phone Number          Visitor's contact number
  Email Address         Visitor's email
  Enquiry Source        Landing page or campaign
  Enquiry Status        New, Contacted, Follow-up, Closed
  Assigned Counsellor   Staff member handling the lead
  Follow-up Date        Planned next contact
  Remarks               Internal follow-up notes

Security and reliability:

-   Do not put Google account passwords, private keys or service-account
    credentials in frontend code.
-   Do not collect more personal information than necessary.
-   Add server-side validation and basic abuse protection.
-   Avoid exposing the spreadsheet to public editing.
-   Restrict access to the spreadsheet to authorised GIS personnel.
-   Publish a privacy notice and define an appropriate data-retention
    process.
-   Handle failed submissions clearly; do not display a success message
    unless the lead has actually been recorded.
-   Consider spam protection and duplicate-submission handling.

## 9. Technical implementation guidance

The developer may choose a suitable modern stack, but should keep the
site lightweight, accessible and easy for GIS staff to update.

A suitable implementation could use:

-   Semantic HTML.
-   CSS with responsive layout rules and design tokens.
-   JavaScript or a modern frontend framework for interactions.
-   CSS transitions and/or a lightweight animation library.
-   Google Apps Script for the Google Sheets lead endpoint.
-   WhatsApp click-to-chat for the enquiry handoff.

Implementation expectations:

-   Reusable components for header, buttons, cards, FAQ accordion and
    form.
-   Centralised configuration for contact details and integration URLs.
-   Optimised images in modern formats where possible.
-   Lazy loading for below-the-fold images.
-   Correct image dimensions to reduce layout shift.
-   Meaningful page title and meta description.
-   Open Graph metadata for social sharing.
-   Favicon using approved GIS brand assets.
-   Accessible labels, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators and
    appropriate colour contrast.
-   Form validation and clear loading, success and error states.
-   No non-functional buttons or placeholder links in the final release.
